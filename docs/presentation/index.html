You stay in control

Keep working. Stay in control.

Cancel a watch, pause the conversation, or leave the terminal. Know what each action does.

What does Esc do?

Esc interrupts the foreground turn. Independent watches and reminders remain registered within their bounds. Say “Stop watching that run” to cancel its observation.

Does stopping a watch stop the work?

Stopping the selected watch ends future observation/admission and forbids renewal or replacement. The observed session or job continues. Already handled messages or actions cannot be undone.

What if a watch expires?

Expiry ends an observation lease or event allowance; it does not prove task completion. The model can explicitly continue useful observation under the existing request, or tell you it ended.

Can I detach the TUI?

You can detach while the owning daemon remains available. If the daemon or source becomes unavailable, Codex Plus reports the gap rather than silently starting a replacement conversation.

Can it reach another host or a subagent?

Ordinary peer coordination is between independent loaded sessions within one daemon. Children are excluded from ordinary selection. Two installed hosts do not add cross-host native messaging.

What if a source needs access?

Your normal tool permissions and configured connections still apply. If a source is unavailable, Codex Plus explains what’s missing. Watching something does not give it permission to change that work.
