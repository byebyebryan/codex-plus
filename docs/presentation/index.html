03 / Your controls

Interrupt, stop and leave.

These are different actions. Select a question to see what the preview does and where its boundary sits.

What does Esc do?

Esc interrupts the foreground turn. Independent watches and reminders remain registered within their bounds. Say “Stop watching that run” to cancel its observation.

Does stopping a watch stop the work?

Stopping the selected watch ends future observation/admission and forbids renewal or replacement. The observed session or job continues. Already handled messages or actions cannot be undone.

What if a watch expires?

Expiry ends an observation lease or event allowance; it does not prove task completion. The model can explicitly continue useful observation under the existing request, or tell you it ended.

Can I detach the TUI?

Detachment does not itself cancel bounded observation while the owner remains available. Lost owners or sources produce a hold/gap; there is no silent replacement or cold resume. Native /exit confirmation interception is not supplied.

Can it reach another host or a subagent?

Ordinary peer coordination is between independent loaded sessions within one daemon. Children are excluded from ordinary selection. Two installed hosts do not add cross-host native messaging.

What if a source needs access?

Commands inherit the verified session grant; configured MCP inspection uses the existing native connection and approval path. Missing access is explained. Observation data cannot authorize new repairs, scaling or work.

What daily use still needs to establish

Real credentials/provider recovery, longer unattended operation and personal UX. The preview supplies no recurring calendar, built-in WebSocket adapter, cross-host native transport or native /exit confirmation dialog. Uncertain delivery is exposed and never blindly replayed.
